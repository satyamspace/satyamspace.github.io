About Me

I’m a GIS and remote sensing specialist with a Master’s degree, passionate about turning complex spatial data into actionable insights. My expertise spans satellite imagery, GeoAI, SAR, hyperspectral analysis, LiDAR, and advanced image interpretation, along with geosurveying and point cloud processing whether in the field or through digital analysis. I help startups, organizations, and research teams solve real-world challenges by delivering high-quality geospatial solutions, from environmental monitoring and sustainability projects to innovative applications in conservation and spatial analytics. I thrive at the intersection of research, applied problem-solving, and practical GIS solutions, always seeking to leverage emerging technologies to uncover hidden patterns and insights.
