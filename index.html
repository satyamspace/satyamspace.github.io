About Me

I’m a final semester GIS student passionate about exploring the next big breakthroughs in geospatial science. I enjoy tackling complex spatial challenges, visualizing intricate environmental phenomena, and applying analytical approaches to uncover hidden stories of our planet. I’ve built strong expertise across the geospatial spectrum, from satellite data analysis, GeoAI, SAR, and hyperspectral processing to LiDAR and advanced image interpretation. I’m also highly skilled in geosurveying and point cloud processing—whether that’s working hands-on in the field or analyzing data on the computer. This versatility allows me to move seamlessly between research-driven analysis, applied problem-solving, and practical geospatial solutions. Currently, I freelance and collaborate with startups as a remote sensing expert, where I combine environmental knowledge with applied GIS skills to support sustainability, conservation, and innovative geospatial applications. I’m always eager to learn, experiment, and stay ahead of emerging trends, whether through projects, collaborations, or new technologies.
